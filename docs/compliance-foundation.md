# Automation Compliance Foundation

The centralized compliance gate is mandatory for delivery, routing, recovery, and consumer-contact automation. It is designed to accept tenant- and vertical-specific decisions for consent, contact restrictions, suppression, DNC, permitted data use, industry limitations, and geography.

Release 8 does not claim that a particular workflow satisfies a legal regime. Compliance rules require qualified assessment during Commercial Readiness and tenant onboarding. A missing or failed compliance decision blocks execution.

Automation operates on operational identifiers and aggregate metrics whenever possible. Identity data remains confined to trusted delivery components with the existing dedicated PII authorization boundary.
