# Acquisition experiments

Experiments support landing-page, creative, CTA, and other variants. Each experiment records dates, status, primary metric, secondary metrics, and minimum sample size. Variants may contain visitors, leads, accepted leads, downstream conversions, revenue, and contribution.

When visitors are available, Axis compares lead conversion, revenue per visitor, and contribution per visitor. It also evaluates acceptance and downstream conversion. Without visitors, visitor-based metrics remain unavailable.

A winner is not declared unless at least two variants meet the minimum sample and contain contribution evidence. Eligible variants are ranked by contribution per visitor, not lead conversion alone. Stale evidence reduces confidence.
