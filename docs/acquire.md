# Axis Acquire

Axis Acquire answers a more important question than which campaign buys the cheapest lead: which acquisition investment creates valuable, recoverable, capacity-compatible downstream outcomes.

The dashboard connects impressions and clicks from media platforms with Axis-attributed leads, validation, delivery acceptance, recovery, CRM outcomes, revenue, and contribution. Missing spend, visitor, revenue, or delivery-cost inputs remain unavailable.

## Scorecards

- Source compares media accounts and platforms.
- Campaign compares cost, acceptance, recovery, downstream conversion, revenue, and contribution.
- Ad Group normalizes Meta Ad Sets and Google/TikTok Ad Groups.
- Creative combines CTR/CPC with lead quality and downstream economics.
- Program rolls campaign metrics into program economics and buyer-capacity context.

Creative fatigue is deterministic: paired CTR decline, CPC increase, and frequency increase signals are compared with configured thresholds. It is a review signal, not an ML claim or automatic creative action.
